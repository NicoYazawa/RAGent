# Travel Expenses Policy

This fictional policy is sample data for the RAGent English README demonstration.

## Requesting a Trip

Business trips must be approved by the employee's manager before departure. The
request should include the destination, the purpose, the estimated travel dates,
and the estimated budget.

## Reimbursement

Employees submit travel claims within ten working days of returning from a trip.
Each claim must list the date, the expense category, the amount, and the receipt.

The following categories are reimbursed:

- Transport: economy rail and economy flights, plus airport transfers.
- Accommodation: up to 600 per night in a standard city and 900 in a capital city.
- Meals: 120 per day, without itemised receipts.
- Other approved costs, such as conference registration fees.

## Not Reimbursable

Personal entertainment, minibar charges, travel insurance upgrades, and damages to
company equipment are not reimbursed. Claims above 5000 must be approved by the
finance team before the trip is booked.
