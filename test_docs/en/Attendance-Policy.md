# Attendance Policy

This fictional policy is sample data for the RAGent English README demonstration.

## Working Hours

The standard workday starts at 9:00 AM and ends at 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
Employees record their arrival and departure through the company attendance application.

## Late Arrival

An arrival after 9:00 AM is recorded as late. Arrivals up to 30 minutes late receive
an attendance reminder. Arrivals more than 30 minutes late must be reported to the
employee's manager on the same day.

Employees should notify their manager before the start of the workday if they
expect to be late. The notification should include the estimated arrival time.

## Attendance Corrections

If an employee cannot clock in because of an application or device issue, they
must submit an attendance correction within two working days. The request should
include the date, actual arrival time, and reason for the missing record.
The employee's manager reviews and approves the correction.

## Planned Absence

Employees submit planned leave requests through the leave application at least
three working days before the requested absence. Emergency leave should be
reported to the manager as soon as practical.
