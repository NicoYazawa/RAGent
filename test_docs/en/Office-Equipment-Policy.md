# Office Equipment Policy

This fictional policy is sample data for the RAGent English README demonstration.

## Requesting Equipment

Every new employee receives a laptop, a monitor, and a keyboard on the first day.
Additional equipment, such as a headset or an external drive, must be requested
through the service desk and approved by the employee's manager.

## Issuance and Return

The service desk records the asset tag of every item it issues. The employee signs
for the equipment in the asset register. Consumables such as paper, pens, and
toner are ordered by the office team each month and do not need a request.

## Replacements

A faulty item is replaced within three working days. The employee returns the
broken item in the original packaging, or disposes of it according to the
instructions of the IT team.
